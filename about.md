### 🔍 Research Interests

Our lab is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of how machines perceive and interact with the world. Our core research spans four key pillars:

* **Multimodal Information Perception**
* **Vision-Language Navigation (VLN)**
* **Remote Sensing Image Processing**
* **Medical Image Processing**

---

### 👥 Lab Members

Our research group is remarkably interdisciplinary. Our interests span statistics, physics, applied mathematics, cognitive science, and many other disciplines. 

We believe that breakthrough research happens at the intersection of these fields. Visit our [People Page](https://mipvn.github.io/people/) to see more information on each person who works in the lab, including their specific publications and contact information.

### 🌿 Lab Culture

Our lab is a vibrant spot for researchers who are **curiosity-driven** and thrive in a fast-paced intellectual environment. Instead of a rigid top-down structure, we encourage autonomy:

* **Individual Ownership:** Everyone is generally the chief of their own individual projects, fostering independence and creative leadership.
* **Dynamic Communication:** We use a variety of practices to facilitate high-quality communication, including weekly chats about lab practices and collaborative brainstorming.
* **Continuous Learning:** We regularly share new tools, coding techniques, and research insights to keep the group at the cutting edge of AI and CV.
