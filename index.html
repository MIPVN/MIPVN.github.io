Welcome to MIPVN Lab!

The Multimodal Information Perception and Visual Navigation Lab is a leading research group directed by Prof. Xiaoyan Luo. We are part of the Image Processing Center at the School of Astronautics, Beihang University, China.

Research Focus

  • 🔹 Multimodal Information Perception
  • 🔹 Vision-Language Navigation (VLN)
  • 🔹 Remote Sensing Image Processing
  • 🔹 Medical Image Processing

Recruitment

We are looking for self-motivated graduate students and undergraduate interns with a passion for innovation. Our lab provides a supportive environment with generous research subsidies, performance bonuses, and fully funded opportunities for domestic and international academic exchanges.
